Introduction

About Me

I am P. Pushpalatha, Assistant Professor of Biotechnology at Government Degree College for Women (Autonomous), Nalgonda, Telangana. My academic work includes teaching, curriculum development, student project guidance, departmental coordination, and research interests that connect biotechnology with health, agriculture, and environmental sustainability.

I am committed to creating engaging learning experiences, encouraging scientific curiosity, and helping students connect core biotechnology concepts with practical and emerging applications.
