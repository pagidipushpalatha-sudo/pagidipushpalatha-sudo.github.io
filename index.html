Introduction

About Me

I am P. Pushpalatha, Assistant Professor of Biotechnology at Government Degree College for Women (Autonomous), Nalgonda, Telangana. My academic work includes teaching, curriculum development, student project guidance, departmental coordination, and research interests connecting biotechnology with health, agriculture, and environmental sustainability.

I aim to create engaging learning experiences, encourage scientific curiosity, and help students connect core biotechnology concepts with practical and emerging applications.
